Capture setup

Saves full UDP packets on the game's ports in a separate 2 GB capture buffer; the oldest packets are overwritten if it fills. Packet files contain network addresses and raw payloads. Stop and wait for conversion before exporting; send the JSONL and its companion .packets folder together. One game per packet capture.

Ctrl+Shift+F5: our vote started · F6: our vote failed · F7: our surrender accepted · F8: enemy vote started · F9: enemy surrender accepted · F10: game ended. These only add your observations to the log.

Sources & coverage

Live events at the selected interval; full game data every 2 s; client phase every 1 s; log tail every 500 ms. Request time adds to these intervals. Unavailable sources and gaps are recorded.

The file contains redacted events, game snapshots and changes, relevant client payloads, route/schema discovery, matching new log lines and your markers. Initial history and schema names are not early detections.
