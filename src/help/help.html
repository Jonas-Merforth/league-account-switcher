Getting started

  1. Click + Add account. Enter a label (for example "Main"), your Riot username and region. A password is optional.
  2. Sign in to that account in the Riot Client with Stay signed in checked.
  3. Click Capture on the account. The app closes the Riot Client and saves the session.
  4. Do the same for your other accounts.

Now Switch signs you in to any account without typing anything. The app closes the Riot Client, restores that account's saved session and starts it again.

Never use Riot's "Sign out" button. Just close the client. Signing out cancels the saved session, so the next switch can't sign you in. If it happens, sign in again and click Capture.

When Friends renews a saved session, Accounts remembers when Riot accepted it. Signed in to Riot Client refers to the account currently open on your PC. If a saved session stops working, sign in again and click Capture. With a saved password, Switch can sign in for you.

Switching

  • Switch signs in as this account.
  • Capture saves the account's current session. It closes the Riot Client to do so.
  • Edit changes the label, username, password or region.
  • Delete removes the account and its saved session.

Drag cards to reorder them. Use + Add section to group accounts, and click a section header to collapse it.

You can also switch from the tray: right-click the tray icon and pick an account. Closing the window keeps the app running in the tray. Use Quit in the tray menu to exit.

The Porofessor button opens your live game. The ... menu has OP.GG, Stats (logins and games per account), Check for updates and this guide.

Queue and champion select

  • Auto Accept accepts every ready check for you. Green means on. Set the wait with Auto accept delay in Settings (0 to 10 seconds, default 2).
  • Auto Lock-In locks the champion you are hovering at the end of your pick turn. Under Champion lock-in in Settings you can lock with 3, 5 or 10 seconds left, or instantly. If nothing is hovered, the pick stays open.
  • Appear offline (eye icon) sets League chat to offline. Turn it on while signed in to go offline now, or with no client open so the next account you switch to logs in offline. Switching again turns it off. A slashed gold eye means you appear offline.
  • Play sound notifications in Settings plays a sound when a queue is accepted or someone dodges. Customize sounds lets you use your own files.

Friends

The Friends tab shows the friends of several of your accounts in one list. The app reads each account's saved session in the background, so nothing gets switched or closed.

  • Sources picks which accounts to include.
  • Mobile also shows friends who are only on the Riot mobile app. Offline shows offline friends.
  • Auto refresh updates the list on a timer. Refresh updates it now.
  • The small account pills show which of your accounts each friend is on. Click one to switch to that account.
  • Join joins a friend's open lobby. Invite invites them to yours.
  • Click the star to keep a friend at the top.
  • Hover a friend who is in a match to see their score (needs Live client features).

If an account shows as failed, its saved session can't be reused. Click Fix failed sessions and the app signs in to each one again.

Hands off while it fixes. The app types each login one after another. Don't use the mouse or keyboard until it's done. If a captcha appears, solve it and it continues.

Queue Relay

If the lobby leader also uses this app, Start via leader starts matchmaking for the lobby. As leader, you can turn this off with the Queue Relay switch.

Chat

Click Chat on a friend, or right-click them, and pick which of your accounts to chat from. Chats with the same friend from different accounts stay separate.

  • The account you chat from comes online and goes offline again after the Chat online timeout (3 minutes by default, change it in Settings).
  • Unread messages show as a number on the Chat tab.
  • Drafts and recent messages are saved on your PC.

Damage Preview

The Damage tab tracks your damage while you play. Click Start tracking before a match. Windows asks for administrator permission.

  • Always on tracks every match until you stop. Next game tracks one match.
  • Tracked game shows your total damage and what dealt it.
  • Team damage compares you with everyone in the match. Full match counts all damage, Takedowns only damage on kills. Hover the badges for details.
  • Healing shows how much each champion heals and suggests anti-heal when needed.
  • Latest fight, Damage taken and Damage dealt break it down further.
  • Gold compares team gold and every opposing lane live (with a Compact view for just you vs your lane opponent).
  • Pick an older game under Tracked game to look at it again.

Drag the cards to change their order. Turn off Live client features in Settings to hide this tab.

Settings

Click the gear icon to open Settings.

  • Default region is used for new accounts.
  • Start with Windows starts the app in the tray. Auto update installs new versions on its own.
  • Aggressive friend fetching loads all Friends sources at once. Faster, but heavier.

Sync settings across accounts

League saves keybinds, camera and video/audio settings separately for every account. With this on, the app keeps them the same everywhere.

  1. Sign in to the account with the settings you want, and set everything up.
  2. Turn on Sync settings across accounts. Your current settings become the shared ones.
  3. Changes are saved after each game and carried over when you switch. Update baseline saves them right away.

Rune pages and item sets stay separate per account. If you sign in to another account outside this app, it offers to Apply now.

Auto-clean client notifications

Claims Season and Mayhem pass rewards and clears the red dots in League (home, Collection, TFT, profile and the bell). It runs in the background and pauses during champion select and games.

  • Clean up now runs it once.
  • Some dots that are already showing only go away after you restart League. Deep-clean visible dots clears them now, while League is idle.

Troubleshooting

  • It started typing my login. The saved session was missing or expired, so the app used your password. Click Capture afterwards to save a fresh session.
  • A captcha appeared. Solve it in the Riot Client. The switch continues after.
  • Stuck on the sign-in screen. The saved session was rejected, usually after "Sign out". Sign in yourself, then click Capture.
  • "Switching would close it". You are in champion select or a game. Finish first, or use Force switch.
  • Something else. Right-click the tray icon, choose Open logs, and send the file along with your report.

Passwords, saved sessions and chat history are encrypted with Windows' built-in protection. Only your Windows user on this PC can read them, and nothing is uploaded. The app only talks to Riot and to the Riot Client and League running on your PC.
